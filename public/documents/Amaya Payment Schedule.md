# Amaya by Vera Vita — Payment Schedule & Pricing Milestone Framework
**Developer**: Vera Vita Living | **RERA Registration No**: TG RERA P02200011109  
**Bank Approvals**: SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank  

---

### Chapter 1: Standard Pricing Components & Structure
§ 1.1 **Basic Sale Price (BSP)**: ₹8,999 per sq.ft of total super built-up area across all towers and configurations.
§ 1.2 **Car Parking Slot**: ₹2,50,000 per designated covered basement or podium parking space.
§ 1.3 **Club Amaya & Amenities Charges**: Includes life membership to Club Amaya, high-speed lift provisioning, DG backup share, piped gas infrastructure, and municipal water connections.
§ 1.4 **Corpus Fund (Sinking Reserve)**: ₹200 per sq.ft of super built-up area payable at handover towards long-term capital replacements.
§ 1.5 **Advance Maintenance Charges**: 12 months advance CAM charges payable at the time of key handover.
§ 1.6 **Applicable Taxes**:
- Goods and Services Tax (GST): 5% applicable on construction value.
- Registration & Stamp Duty: 7.5% payable directly to the Registration & Stamps Department of Telangana upon execution of Sale Deed.
- Tax Deducted at Source (TDS): 1% deductible by buyer under Section 194-IA on total consideration exceeding ₹50 Lakhs.

### Chapter 2: Construction-Linked Payment Milestones (10 Stages)
§ 2.1 **Milestone 1 — Booking Token Advance**: 10% of total unit agreement value payable on submission of Application Form / Booking.
§ 2.2 **Milestone 2 — Agreement of Sale**: 10% payable within 30 days of booking upon execution and formal registration of Agreement of Sale.
§ 2.3 **Milestone 3 — Foundation & Raft**: 10% payable on completion of foundation, piling, and raft slab of the respective tower.
§ 2.4 **Milestone 4 — Basements**: 10% payable on completion of lower and upper basement roof slabs.
§ 2.5 **Milestone 5 — 3rd Floor Roof Slab**: 10% payable on completion of casting of the 3rd floor slab.
§ 2.6 **Milestone 6 — 6th Floor Roof Slab**: 10% payable on completion of casting of the 6th floor slab.
§ 2.7 **Milestone 7 — 9th Floor Roof Slab**: 10% payable on completion of casting of the 9th floor slab.
§ 2.8 **Milestone 8 — 12th Floor Roof / Terrace Slab**: 10% payable on completion of the top roof terrace slab of the respective tower.
§ 2.9 **Milestone 9 — Internal Brickwork & Plastering**: 10% payable on completion of internal masonry, flooring, and external plastering of the unit.
§ 2.10 **Milestone 10 — Handover & Possession**: 10% balance payable on notice of possession along with registration fee, stamp duty, corpus fund, and statutory charges.

### Chapter 4: Banking, Home Loans & Cancellation Policies
§ 4.1 **Approved Financial Institutions**: Amaya is pre-approved by leading institutional lenders including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank for housing loans up to 80% of property cost, including senior citizen co-applicant loan structures.
§ 4.2 **Token Refund Policy**: Token amounts paid at initial inquiry are 100% refundable within 15 calendar days if the applicant opts not to proceed prior to the execution of the Agreement of Sale.
§ 4.3 **Escrow Account**: As mandated by TG RERA, 70% of all customer remittances are maintained in a dedicated project escrow account designated exclusively for Amaya construction and land development.
