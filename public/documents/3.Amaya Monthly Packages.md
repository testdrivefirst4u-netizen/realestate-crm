# Amaya by Vera Vita — Monthly Assisted Living & Maintenance Packages
**Service Provider**: Vera Vita Senior Living Hospitality & Healthcare Management  
**Applicability**: All Residents & Occupants of Towers A, B, and C  

---

### Chapter 1: Estate Management & Common Services (Base CAM)
§ 1.1 **Common Area Maintenance (CAM)** covers 24/7 security guard forces, perimeter CCTV surveillance, concierge desk, landscaping maintenance of 47,000 sq.ft gardens, lift AMCs, diesel power backup operation, and water purification plant maintenance.
§ 1.2 **Internal Campus Mobility**: Unlimited access to electric buggy transportation shuttling residents between towers, Clubhouse Amaya, the medical pavilion, and gate security.
§ 1.3 **Civic Infrastructure**: Common solar-assisted outdoor lighting, solid waste segregation and composting, STP maintenance, and swimming pool filtration.

---

### Chapter 2: Housekeeping, Sanitization & Laundry Services
§ 2.1 **Daily Apartment Housekeeping**: Professional housekeeping team cleans each residence daily (dusting, sweeping, wet-mopping with anti-bacterial eco-friendly solutions, trash clearance).
§ 2.2 **Weekly Deep Cleaning**: Thorough scrubbing of all bathrooms, sanitization of kitchen surfaces, exhaust fans, and dusting of ceiling fixtures.
§ 2.3 **Linen & Personal Laundry**: Bi-weekly bed linen, towels, and pillow cover wash turnaround. Personal clothes washing and steam ironing concierge service delivered directly to apartment wardrobes.
§ 2.4 **Handyman & Maintenance**: In-house electrical, plumbing, carpentry, and AC preventive maintenance available upon request at subsidized rates.

---

### Chapter 3: Healthcare, Geriatric Nursing & Emergency Services
§ 3.1 **24/7 On-Campus Medical Staff**: Certified geriatric registered nurses on duty 24 hours a day, 365 days a year at the Amaya Healthcare Pavilion.
§ 3.2 **Emergency Response**: Immediate in-residence response to panic alarms (pull cords in bathrooms and bedside buttons) within 3 minutes by triage medical team.
§ 3.3 **Campus Ambulance**: Standby Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulance stationed full-time on campus with paramedic driver for rapid transfer to MediCiti Multi-speciality Hospital (12 mins away).
§ 3.4 **Routine Health Monitoring**: Regular vital checks (blood pressure, pulse rate, oxygen levels, and random blood glucose tests) recorded in the resident's confidential digital health record.
§ 3.5 **Doctor Visits**: Weekly visits by general geriatric physicians and monthly specialty consultations (cardiology, orthopedics, neurology).
§ 3.6 **Pharmacy & Diagnostic Logistics**: Scheduled doorstep delivery of routine prescription medicines and home sample collection for diagnostic lab tests.
§ 3.7 **Physiotherapy & Mobility**: Access to on-site licensed physiotherapist for posture, balance recovery, arthritis therapy, and rehabilitation.

---

### Chapter 4: Dining & Nutritional Care Packages
§ 4.1 **Dining Philosophy**: Food curated by master chefs and certified geriatric nutritionists, focusing on wholesome, low-sodium, low-oil, and diabetic-appropriate gourmet Indian cuisine.
§ 4.2 **Pure Vegetarian Food Package**:
- Rate: ₹12,500 per resident per month (plus applicable GST).
- Meals Provided: Morning bed tea/coffee with biscuits, hot buffet breakfast, 4-course hot lunch, evening tea with traditional freshly prepared savories, and wholesome dinner.
§ 4.3 **Non-Vegetarian Food Package**:
- Rate: ₹15,000 per resident per month (plus applicable GST).
- Meals Provided: Full vegetarian spectrum plus curated chicken, fish, and egg preparations served 4 to 5 times weekly.
§ 4.4 **Dietary Customization**: Salt-free, diabetic-friendly, pure Jain, gluten-free, and doctor-prescribed therapeutic diets accommodated at zero extra charge.
§ 4.5 **Guest Dining**: Visiting family and grandchildren can dine at the Clubhouse restaurant on a pay-per-meal basis or purchase weekly guest meal vouchers.
