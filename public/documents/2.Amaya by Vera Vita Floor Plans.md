§ 5.2 **Flooring**: 800 x 800 mm matte finish anti-skid vitrified tiles across living, dining, kitchen, and bedrooms. Non-slippery rustic ceramic tiles in all balconies and washrooms.
§ 5.3 **Doors & Windows**: Main door made of 8-foot seasoned teak wood frame with designer veneered shutter and biometric digital lock. Internal doors with laminated flush shutters and senior-friendly ergonomic lever handles. Sound-insulated double-glazed UPVC sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
§ 5.4 **Bathrooms**: Premium Kohler / Grohe sanitaryware and CP fittings. Wall-hung EWCs with soft-close seats and dual flush. Heavy-duty stainless steel grab rails at toilet and shower areas. Anti-scald thermostatic diverters with preset temperature limits.
§ 5.5 **Electrical & Smart Safety**: Concealed copper wiring with Legrand / Schneider modular switches. Panic buttons beside master bed and bathroom. 100% full DG backup power with automatic transfer switch (ATS).

### Chapter 6: Site Plan, Tower Layout and Residence Numbering (Floor Plans brochure, Oct 2026)
§ 6.1 **Site**: north is up on the plans. The three towers stand in a row at the west end of the 13-acre masterplan, beside the 700-acre Kandlakoya Reserve Forest — Tower A (west, forest side), Tower B (middle), Tower C (east). East of the towers lie the landscaped gardens (meditation zone, sports area, putting green, nature walks, shaded gazebos, north-east water feature), then the future-development land, the Vera Vita Experience Centre and the site entry at the east end. 9 m driveways run north and south of the towers; a landscaped passage links the blocks.
§ 6.2 **Each tower** is a north–south slab with a west face and an east face of residences either side of the lift lobby and fire lift. A garden courtyard with sunken seating sits between Towers A and B; the swimming-pool courtyard between Towers B and C; deck & hobby gardens at the south end of both courtyards.
§ 6.3 **Floors**: Ground — drop-offs, reception and entrance lobby, lift lobbies, parking entries, outdoor amphitheatres, café, yoga room. First floor — Club Amaya (35,000+ sq ft): multipurpose hall, doctor's apartment and chamber, guest rooms, art & craft room, yoga deck, sunken seating, cards room, changing rooms, swimming pool, indoor games, karaoke, kids' playroom, movie hall, golf simulator, library and co-working, banquet, dining, gym, physiotherapy, salon, spa & steam. The first floor of Tower A also holds three forest-side residences: A101 (3.5 BHK), A102 and A103 (3 BHK). Floors 2–12 are typical residential floors; the towers are G + 12.
§ 6.4 **Typical floor** — Tower A (7 homes): west face, north to south, 3.5 BHK-A · 3 BHK · 3 BHK; east face 2.5 BHK-A · 1 BHK-B · 2 BHK-B · 2.5 BHK-B. Towers B and C (8 homes each): west face 2.5 BHK-A · 1 BHK-A · 2 BHK-A · 3 BHK; east face 2.5 BHK-A · 1 BHK-B · 2 BHK-B · 2.5 BHK-B. Totals: Tower A 80, Tower B 88, Tower C 88 = 256 homes.
§ 6.5 **Numbering**: numbers run north to south; odd numbers are on the east face and even numbers on the west face (x01 east row 1, x02 west row 1, x03 east row 2 …). Example: B-704 is the second home from the north on the west face of Tower B's 7th floor (1 BHK-A); C-1201 is the north-east corner of Tower C's 12th floor (2.5 BHK-A).
§ 6.6 **Facing and outlook**: balconies face the home's own side. West-face homes face west — Tower A towards the reserve forest, Tower B towards the A–B courtyard garden, Tower C towards the pool courtyard. East-face homes face east — Tower A towards the A–B courtyard, Tower B towards the pool courtyard, Tower C towards the east gardens. The 3 BHK has a wraparound balcony; the 3.5 BHK-A has two full balconies with forest views on higher floors. Views are never promised — invite a visit.
