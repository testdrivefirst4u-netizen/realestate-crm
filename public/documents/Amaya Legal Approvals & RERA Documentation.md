### Chapter 1: TG RERA Registration & Project Sanctions
§ 1.1 **TG RERA Registration Certificate**: Amaya Phase 1 is registered under TG RERA with official registration number **TG RERA P02200011109**, valid through December 31, 2028.
§ 1.2 **Approved Project Layout**: Sanctioned building plans for Towers A, B, and C (Ground + 12 upper residential floors plus 2 basements) issued by the competent municipal planning authorities.
§ 1.3 **Mandatory Escrow Protection**: 70% of all customer receivables deposited directly into project escrow account in accordance with Section 4(2)(l)(D) of Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016.

### Chapter 2: Land Title, Encumbrance & Ownership
§ 2.1 **Clear Marketable Freehold Title**: The project land measuring 13 Acres (Phase 1 comprising 3 Acres) in Munirabad Village possesses an indisputable, clear marketable title held in fee simple, investigated and certified through 30-year non-encumbrance legal search by senior High Court advocates.
§ 2.2 **Non-Agricultural Conversion**: Legal non-agricultural land conversion order granted by the Revenue Department / District Collectorate for development of residential senior living habitat.
§ 2.3 **Nil Encumbrance Certificate**: Nil encumbrance certified on public record up to the present date with the Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) Medchal.

---

### Chapter 3: Statutory NOCs & Environmental Clearances
§ 3.1 **State Environmental Impact Assessment Authority (SEIAA) Clearance**: Full Environmental Clearance (EC) granted for comprehensive project execution including water recycling, rainwater recharge, and low-emission building standards.
§ 3.2 **Telangana State Disaster Response & Fire Services Department**: Provisional and construction Fire NOC issued for high-rise towers with dual fire escape stairs, pressurized refuge areas, and wet riser systems.
§ 3.3 **Airports Authority of India (AAI)**: Height clearance certificate obtained certifying allowable building height up to 48.5 meters above ground level.
§ 3.4 **Groundwater Authority & PCB Consents**: Consent for Establishment (CFE) issued by Telangana State Pollution Control Board for sewage treatment plant (STP) and water conservation.

---

### Chapter 4: Senior Living Model Agreement & Resident Welfare Bylaws
§ 4.1 **Model Agreement Compliance**: Sale deeds and maintenance agreements formatted in strict compliance with the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) Senior Citizens Housing Guidelines.
§ 4.2 **Resident Age Eligibility**: Primary registered occupant or at least one joint occupant must be 55 years of age or older at the time of occupancy. Visited stays by adult children, grandchildren, and caregivers permitted up to 60 consecutive days.
§ 4.3 **Dispute Resolution & Resident Association**: Formal provision for an elected Amaya Resident Welfare Association (ARWA) with joint oversight of hospitality, food quality, safety, and escrow-backed maintenance fund audit.
