# Amaya by Vera Vita — Master Project Overview & Brochure
**TG RERA Registration No: TG RERA P02200011109**  
**Developer**: Vera Vita Living | **Architecture**: Abin Design Studio  
**Location**: Munirabad Village, Medchal Mandal, Hyderabad, Telangana  

---

### Chapter 1: Introduction & Master Development Vision
§ 1.1 Amaya by Vera Vita is Hyderabad’s landmark luxury senior living sanctuary, conceived across a 13-acre private green enclave in Medchal. Phase 1 spans 3 pristine acres featuring three residential towers (Towers A, B, and C) rising Ground + 12 floors with 256 master-crafted residences.
§ 1.2 Designed in collaboration with the award-winning Abin Design Studio, Amaya departs from clinical care environments to celebrate vibrant, active aging, multi-generational familial visits, and holistic wellness.
§ 1.3 Every square foot embodies 100% Vaastu compliance, uninhibited universal access, zero-threshold flooring, anti-skid vitrified tiles, and expansive views over the adjacent 700-acre Kandlakoya Reserve Forest.

### Chapter 2: Strategic Location & Connectivity
§ 2.1 Amaya is positioned in Munirabad Village, Medchal, offering pristine air quality index (AQI) levels significantly superior to central urban Hyderabad while retaining rapid transit connections.

§ 2.3 Arterial Connectivity: Located only 10 minutes from Outer Ring Road (ORR Exit 6), giving seamless signal-free access across Hyderabad.

### Chapter 4: Universal Senior Engineering & Safety Standards
§ 4.1 Zero-Threshold Design: Flush floor transitions between living, bedroom, balcony, and wet areas to completely eliminate tripping hazards for walkers and wheelchairs.
§ 4.2 Doorway Clearances: 4-foot wide main entrances and 3.5-foot wide internal doors accommodating motorized wheelchairs and emergency stretcher beds.
§ 4.3 Bathroom Safeguards: Wall-mounted grab rails in European water closet (EWC) and shower zones, fold-down shower seating, thermostatic anti-scald faucets, and emergency pull-cord alarm switches wired directly to the 24/7 campus nurse station.
§ 4.4 Master Bedroom Alarms: Panic buttons installed beside both bed heads with immediate two-way intercom link to the medical emergency command team.
§ 4.5 Mobility Vertical Transit: Two high-speed senior-calibrated elevators per tower with slow-closing sensor doors, braille indicators, voice announcements, and emergency battery lowering systems. One stretcher elevator per tower sized for medical emergencies.
§ 4.6 Campus Buggy Service: Fleet of quiet electric golf carts for effortless transport across club, towers, walking gardens, and entrance portal.

### Chapter 5: Healthcare & Medical Infrastructure
§ 5.1 On-Campus Medical Center: Round-the-clock medical care pavilion staffed 24/7 by licensed geriatric registered nurses and emergency medical technicians (EMTs).
§ 5.2 Consulting Physicians: Daily scheduled OPD consultations with visiting geriatric physicians, cardiologists, and orthopedicians.
§ 5.3 Dedicated Campus Ambulance: Fully equipped on-campus Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulance stationed 24/7 at Amaya for instant patient transfer to MediCiti Hospital.
§ 5.4 Daily Vitals Monitoring: Routine monitoring of resident blood pressure, blood glucose, heart rate, and oxygen saturation with digital cloud health passport tracking.
§ 5.5 Pharmacy & Diagnostics: Doorstep medicine delivery via accredited network pharmacies and weekly on-site sample collection by certified diagnostic labs.
§ 5.6 Rehabilitation: Physiotherapy department equipped with ultrasound therapy, electrical stimulation, and joint mobility rehabilitation gear supervised by licensed physiotherapists.

### Chapter 6: Safety, Security & Green Living
§ 6.1 Gated Multi-Tier Security: 24/7 physical security guards, boom barriers, license plate recognition at gates, and comprehensive perimeter infrared boundary monitoring.
§ 6.2 Digital Surveillance: Over 120 high-definition IP cameras covering all public pathways, lobbies, elevators, and clubhouse perimeters.
§ 6.3 Fire Safety: Complete compliance with Telangana Fire Services with automatic sprinkler heads, smoke detectors in every unit, fire hose reels, and external fire escape staircases.
§ 6.4 Power & Water Reliability: 100% generator backup power for all apartments (lights, fans, televisions, refrigerators, and select air conditioners) and common infrastructure. Water softening plant, multi-stage RO drinking water filtration, and rainwater harvesting recharging wells.
